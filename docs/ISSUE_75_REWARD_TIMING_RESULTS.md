## Four new hypotheses

| Test-only mode | Exact change |
|---|---|
| `boss_chest_lag` | Equipment generated by boss chests on floors 1–3 arrives at the following floor's entry; direct boss equipment and every potion remain immediate. |
| `treasure_lock3` | Equipment generated by ordinary treasure chests on floors 1–3 arrives at floor-four entry, with original item budgets; potion rewards remain immediate. |
| `perk_lag` | Portal offers earned on floors 1–3 are offered after the following floor's exit; floor four offers the delayed floor-three card and its normal card. Original floor keys seed the rolls; actual active-unique filtering and policy still apply. No already-selected card is rerolled. |
| `combined_lag` | Apply those three timing changes together to measure their interaction. |

All early cached equipment is delivered by floor four and all six perk offers by final descent. Perk selection at a different time can change the chosen card because current armor/active uniques affect policy; this is disclosed rather than mislabeled an identical-perk counterfactual. The focused regression covers source boundaries, immediate guaranteed potion pickup, repeated delivery, unchanged item budgets, no equip healing, no extra combat RNG and no cross-run pending reward leakage.
